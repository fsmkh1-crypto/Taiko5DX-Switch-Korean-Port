## V275 — KO_FULL_SENTENCE_GRAMMAR_FLATTENING_V1 design disposition

Status: CANONICAL DESIGN DIRECTION

The selected Korean design rule is:

`preserve dynamic meaning / flatten inherited Japanese dynamic grammar`

Required semantic buckets:

```text
GRAMMAR_FLATTEN
DYNAMIC_MEANING_RETAIN
MIXED_SPLIT_REQUIRED
UNRESOLVED
```

A grammar family is not globally blanked first. Required order is full caller census,
semantic/branch classification, caller-level Korean sentence migration, residual caller
accounting, and only then possible retirement of an unused grammar literal family.

Global repeated-syllable dedup, suffix deletion, forced predicate values, global
speech-style inversion, and C73/C188 local first-syllable stripping remain rejected.

This design adds no WRITE_SAFE authority.
