### Current design consequence

The core rule remains:

`preserve dynamic meaning / flatten inherited Japanese dynamic grammar`

The tone contract is now stricter:

1. preserve caller-owned `0x6A` and all existing caller control/secondary branches;
2. preserve required dynamic-semantic call IDs/order;
3. flatten inherited Japanese grammar segmentation only after branch-local tone is
   explicitly adjudicated;
4. do not claim nested formatter tone preservation from caller `0x6A` alone;
5. classify meaningful speaker/relationship differences as `TONE_CRITICAL_EXCEPTION`;
6. treat SC/TW as structural evidence only, never Korean wording/tone authority.

The following remain rejected: global repeated-syllable cleanup, fixed prefix/suffix
trimming, global `0x6A` inversion, forced predicate values, early global blanking of the
grammar cluster, automatic treatment of the former 913, automatic treatment of all 567,
and automatic treatment of the 460 root-style callers.
