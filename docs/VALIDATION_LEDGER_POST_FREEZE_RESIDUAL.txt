## 1. Status meanings

- `VERIFIED` — reproducible mechanical fact from fixed canonical evidence; membership-bearing claims have materialized membership/hash or already closed canonical provenance.
- `MECHANISM_OBSERVED` — direct PC patch record/runtime relationship; not Switch authorization.
- `SEMANTIC_REVIEWED` — explicit semantic/context judgment after evidence review.
- `PROVISIONAL` — useful reviewed conclusion not promoted to deterministic Switch authority.
- `SUPERSEDED` — retained historical claim replaced by a later claim.
- `RETRACTED` — retained historical claim found unsound.

Materializing the exact membership of a PROVISIONAL semantic set does not change its semantic status to VERIFIED.
