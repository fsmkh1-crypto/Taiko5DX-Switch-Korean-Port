## 1. Status meanings

This ledger deliberately separates reproducible mechanical facts from occurrence-level PC mechanism observations and semantic review.

- `VERIFIED` — reproducible from fixed canonical evidence/selector or already closed canonical evidence; count/membership claims require the provenance appropriate to that claim.
- `MECHANISM_OBSERVED` — direct inspection of PC patch records/runtime relationship; re-queryable observation, not a semantic or Switch authorization.
- `SEMANTIC_REVIEWED` — explicit human/model semantic-context judgment after evidence review.
- `PROVISIONAL` — useful observation or reviewed count not yet promoted to a canonical artifact/membership.
- `SUPERSEDED` — retained historical claim replaced by a later claim.
- `RETRACTED` — retained historical claim found unsound.

`MECHANISM_OBSERVED` does not imply that the same mechanism must be used on Switch. `PROVISIONAL` may not authorize a write.
