요약

The two original Switch 7,494-entry lookup loops remain unchanged. Each genuine original-table miss is redirected by one fixed-width AArch64 branch to a compact helper in the already-validated TEXT alignment region. The helper resolves only the 2,542 Korean-added keys and otherwise restores the original miss fallback. The forward and reverse paths then re-enter the original function at existing post-miss output points.
